# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Test Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads design and execute test strategies, validate acceptance criteria, and ensure quality standards are met before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans aligned with acceptance criteria and Definition of Done
- Execute unit, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Identify and triage defects and coordinate with developers on fixes
- Validate smoke tests before production deployment
- Report quality metrics, risk signals, and release readiness to PMs and project leaders

### Goals
- Reduce defects that reach production
- Improve confidence in release quality and user impact
- Ensure testability is built into delivery decisions

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and QA signoff meetings
- Defect triage with engineering and PM partners
- Status updates for release readiness and quality risks

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to review acceptance criteria, testability, and defect resolution
- Partners with Product Managers to confirm customer-facing functionality matches expectations
- Keeps Project Managers informed of quality risks, blockers, and release readiness

---

## Engineering Manager / Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers and Tech Leads own technical strategy, team capacity planning, and architectural decisions. They act as a technical escalation point and support engineering execution.

### Responsibilities
- Assess technical feasibility and estimate effort with developers
- Identify and manage technical dependencies and risks
- Lead design decisions, trade-offs, and architecture alignment
- Manage team capacity, sequencing, and delivery health
- Mentor developers and help maintain code quality standards

### Goals
- Deliver technical execution with predictable quality and speed
- Balance engineering capacity with product priorities
- Reduce friction from technical blockers and dependency issues

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning and architecture reviews
- Risk and dependency updates with PMs and Product Managers
- Technical escalation discussions during delivery challenges

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to assess scope trade-offs and technical feasibility
- Coordinates with Project Managers on team capacity, sequencing, and timeline impacts
- Helps Developers resolve technical complexity and maintain delivery quality

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors represent business needs, provide strategic direction, and make key decisions. They act as executive champions for the project.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Approve budget, scope, and resource allocation decisions
- Set strategic priorities and trade-off guidance
- Provide escalation paths for critical decisions or business blockers
- Receive and act on project status, risks, and milestone updates

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable business value
- Support strong alignment between strategy and delivery execution
- Enable timely decisions that keep the initiative moving

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based stakeholder briefings
- Decision reviews and escalation discussions
- High-level status updates and approval checkpoints

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Aligns with Product Managers on priorities, outcomes, and business impact
- Works with Project Managers on approvals, escalations, and governance decisions
- Provides strategic context that helps the team prioritize effectively

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that security requirements, privacy expectations, and regulatory obligations are built into project delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for features and services
- Review architectural decisions and implementation choices for risk exposure
- Oversee security scanning, access controls, and vulnerability management
- Support incident response, mitigation planning, and post-incident learning
- Advise on data protection, privacy, and compliance considerations

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across the project lifecycle
- Ensure delivery decisions include risk controls and mitigation planning
- Enable safe release practices without slowing value delivery unnecessarily

### Typical Communication
- Design review and security signoff checkpoints
- Risk, incident, and remediation updates with PM and engineering leads
- Compliance and audit related discussions with stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Developers and Engineering Managers on secure design and implementation decisions
- Works with Project Managers on risk tracking, mitigation planning, and release gates
- Advises Product Managers and Stakeholders on constraints, trade-offs, and risk decisions
